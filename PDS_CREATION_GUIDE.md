## Overview

The **Personal Data Sheet (PDS)** is the official CS Form 212 (Revised 2025) as prescribed by the Civil Service Commission. It contains comprehensive personal, professional, and background information required for government employment.

### ⚠️ Important Warning
**Any misrepresentation made in the Personal Data Sheet and the Work Experience Sheet shall cause the filing of administrative/criminal case/s against the person concerned.**

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Access the PDS Form
1. Log in to the **Employee Portal**
2. Navigate to **Dashboard → PDS** (or click the PDS link in the sidebar)
3. You will see the full 4-page form divided into sections

### Step 2: Browser Compatibility
- Use **Google Chrome**, **Firefox**, or **Edge** (latest versions)
- Enable JavaScript in your browser
- Recommended screen resolution: 1024×768 or higher

### Step 3: Before You Start
- Have the following documents ready:
  - Birth Certificate
  - Government-issued ID (Passport, Driver's License, GSIS, SSS, PRC License)
  - Educational diplomas/certificates
  - Employment records
  - Training certificates

## Section-by-Section Guide

### **Section I: PERSONAL INFORMATION** (Items 1-21)

#### Names (Items 1-2)
- **Surname**: Write your FULL surname without abbreviations
- **First Name**: Write your FULL first name
- **Middle Name**: Write your FULL middle name
- **Name Extension**: Enter if applicable (Jr., Sr., III)

**Example**: 
- Surname: DELA CRUZ
- First Name: JUAN
- Middle Name: GABRIEL
- Extension: JR.

---

#### Personal Details (Items 3-6)
- **Date of Birth**: Format: DD/MM/YYYY (e.g., 15/05/1990)
- **Place of Birth**: City/Municipality and Province (e.g., Manila, NCR)
- **Sex at Birth**: Select Male or Female
- **Civil Status**: Choose one from:
  - Single
  - Married
  - Widowed
  - Separated
  - Solo Parent
  - Others

---

#### Physical Characteristics (Items 7-9)
- **Height**: In meters (e.g., 1.65m)
- **Weight**: In kilograms (e.g., 60kg)
- **Blood Type**: Select from A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-

---

#### Government ID Numbers (Items 10-15)
Enter the following ID numbers if you have them:
- **Item 10**: UMID ID No.
- **Item 11**: PAG-IBIG ID No.
- **Item 12**: PhilHealth No.
- **Item 13**: PhilSys Number (PSN)
- **Item 14**: TIN No.
- **Item 15**: Agency Employee No.

---

#### Citizenship (Item 16)
- **Filipino**: Select if you are a Filipino citizen
- **Dual Citizenship**: Select if applicable
  - Choose: By Birth or By Naturalization
  - Specify the other country

---

#### Addresses (Items 17-18)

**Residential Address** (Item 17):
- House/Block/Lot Number
- Street
- Subdivision/Village
- Barangay
- City/Municipality
- Province
- ZIP Code

**Permanent Address** (Item 18):
- Check "Same as Residential" if identical
- Otherwise, fill in the same sub-fields

---

#### Contact Information (Items 19-21)
- **Telephone No.**: Landline (if available)
- **Mobile No.**: Cell phone number
- **E-Mail Address**: Active email address

---

### **Section II: FAMILY BACKGROUND** (Items 22-25)

#### Spouse Information (Item 22)
Fill in your spouse's details if married:
- Surname, First Name, Middle Name, Name Extension
- Occupation
- Employer/Business Name
- Business Address
- Telephone No.

---

#### Children (Item 23)
Add each child's information:
- **Full Name**: Write in full
- **Date of Birth**: Format DD/MM/YYYY
- Click "+ Add Child" to add more rows

---

#### Father's Information (Item 24)
- Surname, First Name, Middle Name, Name Extension

---

#### Mother's Maiden Name (Item 25)
- Surname, First Name, Middle Name
- Use maiden name (before marriage)

---

### **Section III: EDUCATIONAL BACKGROUND** (Item 26)

Add education records starting from ELEMENTARY through GRADUATE STUDIES:

**For Each Level, Enter:**
- **Level**: Elementary, Secondary, Vocational, College, or Graduate Studies
- **Name of School**: Write in full
- **Degree/Course**: Write in full (e.g., Bachelor of Science in Education)
- **Period From**: DD/MM/YYYY
- **Period To**: DD/MM/YYYY
- **Highest Level/Units Earned**: If not graduated
- **Year Graduated**: YYYY
- **Honors**: Any academic honors/scholarships received

---

### **Section IV: CIVIL SERVICE ELIGIBILITY** (Item 27)

Add each civil service exam you passed:
- **Eligibility**: CES, CSEE, Career Service, RA 1080, etc.
- **Rating**: Score if applicable
- **Date of Examination**: DD/MM/YYYY
- **Place of Examination**: City/Location
- **License Number**: If applicable
- **License Valid Until**: DD/MM/YYYY

---

### **Section V: WORK EXPERIENCE** (Item 28)

List your work experience starting with the MOST RECENT:

**For Each Position, Enter:**
- **Date From**: DD/MM/YYYY
- **Date To**: DD/MM/YYYY (or "Present" if current)
- **Position Title**: Full title without abbreviation
- **Department/Agency/Company**: Full name
- **Status of Appointment**: Permanent, Temporary, Contractual, etc.
- **Salary Grade/Pay Grade**: Format SG/Step (e.g., SG-11/3)
- **Government Service**: Y or N

---

### **Section VI: VOLUNTARY WORK** (Item 29)

Add your volunteer work experience:
- **Organization Name & Address**: Full details
- **Date From**: DD/MM/YYYY
- **Date To**: DD/MM/YYYY
- **Number of Hours**: Total hours served
- **Position/Nature of Work**: Role or activities

---

### **Section VII: TRAINING & DEVELOPMENT** (Item 30)

List training programs attended:
- **Title**: Training program name
- **Date From**: DD/MM/YYYY
- **Date To**: DD/MM/YYYY
- **Number of Hours**: Duration in hours
- **Type of L&D**: Managerial, Supervisory, Technical, etc.
- **Conducted/Sponsored By**: Organization name

---

### **Section VIII: OTHER INFORMATION** (Items 31-33)

#### 31. Special Skills & Hobbies
Click "+ Add Skill/Hobby" to add entries:
- Examples: Computer skills, languages, hobbies, certifications

#### 32. Non-Academic Distinctions
Click "+ Add Distinction" for recognitions/awards:
- Examples: Employee of the Month, citations, recognitions

#### 33. Membership in Associations/Organizations
Click "+ Add Membership" for professional/civic memberships:
- Examples: Professional associations, clubs, civic organizations

---

### **Section IX: QUESTIONS 34-40** (Integrity Questions)

Answer the following questions honestly:

#### Q34: Relationship to Appointing Authority
- Related within 3rd degree?
- Related within 4th degree (for LGU)? [If YES, provide details]

#### Q35: Administrative/Criminal Charges
- Found guilty of administrative offense? [If YES, provide details]
- Criminally charged? [If YES, provide date filed and status]

#### Q36: Criminal Conviction
- Ever convicted of any crime? [If YES, provide details]

#### Q37: Separation from Service
- Ever separated (resignation, dismissal, etc.)? [If YES, provide details]

#### Q38: Election Candidacy
- Candidate in national/local election within last year? [If YES, provide details]
- Resigned to campaign in 3-month period? [If YES, provide details]

#### Q39: Immigrant/Permanent Resident Status
- Acquired status of immigrant or permanent resident? [If YES, specify country]

#### Q40: Special Group Membership
- Member of indigenous group? [If YES, specify group]
- Person with disability? [If YES, provide PWD ID No.]
- Solo parent? [If YES, provide SP ID No.]

---

### **Section X: REFERENCES** (Item 41)

Provide 3 references (NOT blood relatives):

**For Each Reference:**
- **Name**: Full name
- **Office/Residential Address**: Complete address
- **Contact No./Email**: Phone or email

**Important**: References must NOT be related to you by consanguinity (blood) or affinity (marriage).

---

### **Section XI: DECLARATION** (Item 42)

#### Government ID Information
- **Government ID Type**: Passport, GSIS, SSS, PRC, Driver's License, etc.
- **ID/License/Passport No.**: The number on your ID
- **Date/Place of Issuance**: When and where it was issued

#### Declaration Statement
You are declaring under oath that:
- Information is true, correct, and complete
- You authorize verification of the contents
- Misrepresentation may result in administrative/criminal cases

#### Declaration Signature Block (Required)
- **Signature**: Sign the form
- **Date Accomplished**: Date you completed the form

## Tips & Best Practices

### 📝 Writing Guidelines
✓ **DO:**
- Write LEGIBLY if handwriting
- Use FULL names without abbreviations
- Use DD/MM/YYYY date format
- Be ACCURATE and HONEST
- Provide COMPLETE information

✗ **DON'T:**
- Use nicknames or informal names
- Abbreviate any field
- Leave required fields blank
- Write "N/A" unless truly not applicable
- Make corrections with pen/markers

---

### 💾 Saving Your Work
1. **Save Frequently**: Click "💾 Save PDS" regularly as you fill sections
2. **Auto-save**: The form does NOT auto-save; you must click Save
3. **Confirmation**: You'll see "✅ PDS saved successfully!" message
4. **Verification**: Reload the page to verify your data persists

---

### 🖨️ Printing Your PDS
1. Navigate to the filled PDS form
2. Click "🖨️ Print PDS" button at the bottom
3. A new window opens with the formatted 4-page form
4. Review carefully before printing
5. Use white standard paper (8.5" × 11" or A4)
6. Print in color or B&W

---

### 📋 PDF Generation
To save as PDF:
1. Click "🖨️ Print PDS"
2. In the browser print dialog:
   - Select "Save as PDF" as printer
   - Click Save
   - Choose filename and location

## Important Reminders

### ⏰ Deadlines
- Submit completed PDS within **30 days** of appointment
- Update PDS annually or as required
- Respond to verification requests promptly

### ✅ Before Submitting
1. **Review completely**: Read all 4 pages carefully
2. **Verify accuracy**: Double-check all dates, names, numbers
3. **Check completeness**: Ensure all required fields are filled
4. **Signature**: Sign the declaration section
5. **Get ID ready**: Have government ID for verification

### 🔐 Privacy & Security
- Your PDS contains sensitive personal information
- Do not share your PDS with unauthorized persons
- The HR department will use it for verification only
- Data is protected under Data Privacy Act

### 📞 Support & Help
If you encounter issues:
1. **Contact HR Office**: Contact your HR staff
2. **Technical Issues**: Clear browser cache and reload
3. **Lost Work**: The form saves to database; refresh to reload
4. **Questions**: Refer to this guide or ask HR

### 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Incomplete Information**: Fill ALL required fields
2. **Wrong Date Format**: Use DD/MM/YYYY consistently
3. **Abbreviations**: Write names and titles in FULL
4. **Inconsistent Dates**: Ensure dates don't overlap oddly
5. **Wrong Citizenship Status**: Select correct option
6. **Unrelated References**: Ensure references are not family
7. **Unsigned Declaration**: Always sign Item 42
8. **No ID Information**: Complete Item 42 government ID section

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What if my address changes after submitting?**
A: Update your PDS immediately. Inaccurate information may affect benefits/services.

**Q: Can I edit my PDS after submitting?**
A: Yes, you can update it anytime. Save the revised version.

**Q: What if I don't have all documents right now?**
A: Fill what you know and update later. HR can verify available records.

**Q: How long is PDS valid?**
A: PDS should be current. Update it annually or when information changes.

**Q: Can I print my PDS multiple times?**
A: Yes, anytime. Print for your records or reference.

**Q: What happens if I provide false information?**
A: This is a serious offense that may result in administrative/criminal charges.

**Q: Who can access my PDS?**
A: Only authorized HR personnel. Your PDS is confidential.
